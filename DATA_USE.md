# Data use conditions

The software in this repository is licensed under Apache-2.0. The bundled
anonymised real-data example and deidentified C01-C07 feature cache are not
covered by the software licence.

The data are provided for research reproduction, method development, and
benchmarking. Users must:

1. comply with their institutional ethics, governance, and data-security rules;
2. use the data only for legitimate research and educational purposes;
3. not attempt to identify participants or combine these data with information
   intended to reveal identity;
4. preserve the participant anonymisation and channel-QC decisions; and
5. cite the associated manuscript and repository in resulting work.

Use of these human-derived data must remain consistent with Griffith University
approvals 2023/444 and 2022/762 and the consent conditions applying to the
source studies.

Contact the repository maintainers for access or governance questions.
